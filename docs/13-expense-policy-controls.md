# Expense Policy & Internal Controls

This document outlines the financial policies and compliance controls designed to prevent fraud and ensure budget adherence.

## 1. Expense Approval Policy (Tiered Limits)

| Claim Amount | Approval Required | Receipt Mandatory | Finance Review |
|---|---|---|---|
| Up to ₹5,000 | Auto-Approval (System) | Yes | No |
| ₹5,001 – ₹25,000 | Manager | Yes | No |
| ₹25,001 – ₹1,00,000 | Manager + Dept Head | Yes | Yes |
| Above ₹1,00,000 | Manager + Dept Head + Finance Director | Yes | Yes |
| Client Entertainment | Manager + Finance (Any amount) | Yes | Yes |

## 2. Fraud & Compliance Controls

### A. Preventive Controls (Before Payment)
1. **Mandatory Receipt Rule:** The system will not accept a claim without a digital receipt.
2. **Invoice Matching:** The system will hard-block claims if the same Invoice Number is used twice for the same employee.
3. **Policy Engine & Category Caps:** The system enforces strict limits on high-risk categories. For example, a single Client Entertainment claim cannot exceed ₹15,000, and Travel (Flight) is capped at ₹20,000. Claims above these limits are flagged for Finance review.
### B. Detective Controls (After Payment)
1. **Post-Audit Sampling:** The Finance team will randomly audit 5% of auto-approved claims (<₹5,000) on a monthly basis.
2. **Manager SLA Tracking:** Weekly reports will highlight managers who take more than 3 days to approve claims.

## 3. Segregation of Duties (SoD)
- The person **submitting** the claim (Employee) cannot be the person **approving** it (Manager).
- The person **approving** the claim (Manager) cannot process the **payment** (Finance).
- This prevents a single user from committing and hiding fraud.
